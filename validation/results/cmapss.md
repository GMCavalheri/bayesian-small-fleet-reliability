# Extension: NASA C-MAPSS as four fleets with a known rate

Generated by `validation/cmapss.py`; do not edit by hand.

## Data

Training run-to-failure trajectories of NASA C-MAPSS (Saxena, Goebel, Simon and Eklund 2008), verified by SHA-256. Life is the last cycle of each training engine; the true rate is 1000 / mean life (renewal theorem), per 1000 cycles. FD004's file holds 249 engines, although its readme says 248.

## Setup

Each subset is a fleet of k engine positions, each a stationary renewal process with lives resampled from that subset, observed for a window of cycles. The four subsets are the units of the hierarchical model, with hyperprior μ ~ LogNormal(log 4.0, 1) (a 250-cycle mean life) and cv ~ HalfNormal(1). Seeded Monte Carlo, 300 replicates (seed 20261126); RMSE and coverage pooled over the four subsets, ± one standard error.
