# Empirical Bayes: pooling several small operators of one type

Generated by `validation/empirical_bayes.py`; do not edit by hand.

## Setup

Component `starter_generator`. Each operator's true rate is drawn from a Gamma population with mean 0.25 per 1000 FH and coefficient of variation cv (illustrative assumption: operators differ by sector length, climate and maintenance practice). Operators fly 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12 aircraft (cycled) for 12 months at 35 FH per aircraft-month. Seeded Monte Carlo, 500 replicates per cell; RMSE and coverage are pooled over operators and replicates (standard error of a coverage near 0.9 is at most 0.006). Widths below 0.01 (fits on the zero-spread boundary) are reported as a bound. With cv = 0.5 the population 90 % range is (0.085, 0.485).
