## Setup

The small fleet is 5 business jets observed for 12 months (2,100 FH). The large fleet is 100 narrow-bodies over 36 months (900,000 FH), simulated once at the large-fleet rates (seed 20261119). The power prior is the Jeffreys prior updated with the large fleet's data counted as 10,000 FH (a0 = 0.0111); full borrowing counts all of it (a0 = 1). Every performance number is exact: a sum over the Poisson count at the true small-fleet rate.
