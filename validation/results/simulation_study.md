# Phase 5: when does Bayesian borrowing help?

Generated by `validation/simulation_study.py`; do not edit by hand.

## Setup

One component on business jets (35 FH per aircraft-month) observed for 12 months; the large fleet's rate is 0.2 per 1000 FH and the small fleet's true rate is 1.0, 1.5 or 3.0 times it (good, biased and conflicting prior). The borrowed prior has the large fleet's mean and is worth 10,000 FH; full borrowing counts all 900,000 FH. Single-fleet results are exact (sums over the Poisson count). The hierarchical arm pools 8 operators of the same size whose rates vary with cv 0.5 around the true mean, under μ ~ LogNormal(log 0.2, 1), cv ~ HalfNormal(1); it is seeded Monte Carlo (120 replicates, seed 20261125), reported with standard errors.
