# Phase 4: Bayesian alert thresholds against the classical alert level

Generated by `validation/alerts.py`; do not edit by hand.

## Setup

Component `starter_generator`, true rate 0.25 per 1000 FH, 35 FH per aircraft-month. Each history has 12 baseline months and 12 monitored months; in the shifted histories the rate is 2× from the first monitored month. The Bayesian rule alerts when P(λ > 0.375 | prior, last w months) > p*; the target is 1.5× the nominal rate (an illustrative acceptance limit). Its prior is the large fleet's rate (0.2) counted as 10,000 FH: Gamma(2.5, 10). The classical rule uses mean + kσ of the 12 baseline monthly rates. Seeded Monte Carlo, 4000 replicates per fleet size, same histories for every rule (standard error of a false-alarm rate p is sqrt(p(1−p)/48 000), of a detection probability sqrt(p(1−p)/4000)).
